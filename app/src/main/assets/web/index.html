How to use

  1. Make a sticker: tap New sticker, or share an image to Stix from any app (Chrome, Instagram, Gallery…). Crop it, erase the background if you like, and save it to a group.
  2. GIF stickers: share a GIF (or a link to one, e.g. from Google Images, Tenor or Giphy) to Stix, or tap 🔗 and paste the link. Crop it and save it to a GIF group. WhatsApp keeps animated and still stickers in separate packs.
  3. Groups are sticker packs: once a group has at least 3 stickers, tap Add to WhatsApp. The pack then shows up in WhatsApp's sticker panel.
  4. Changes sync: stickers you add to a pack later appear in WhatsApp automatically (it can take a moment, or reopen the sticker panel).

A pack can hold 3–30 stickers. Each sticker needs 1–3 emojis.
